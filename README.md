## What is this? 🤔

Basically, I got tired of normal, boring websites and decided to turn my site into an entire mini operating system. It looks and feels like a classic hacker terminal mixed with a retro desktop. 

You can drag windows around your screen, check the live clock, launch little apps from the start menu, and vibe out with the cyber theme. No bloated frameworks or crazy dependencies—just raw web basics doing their thing.

---

## Cool Stuff Inside ✨

* **Movable Windows:** Grab any window by the top bar and drag it wherever you want on your screen.
* **Window Focus:** Clicking any window pulls it straight to the front so you don't lose track of what you're doing.
* **Taskbar & Start Menu:** A bottom bar with a real-time ticking clock and a quick menu to open your tools.
* **Built-in Apps:** Comes loaded with a Terminal welcome screen, a DevLogs reader, a handy Notepad to type stuff out, a working Calculator, and a SysInfo screen with neat ASCII art.
